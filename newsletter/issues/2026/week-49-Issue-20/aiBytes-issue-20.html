Hello, AI enthusiasts 👋

Welcome back to your weekly dose of aiBytes_.

Here's a quick update: we've shipped aiBytes_ as a Chrome extension to help you quickly get to the daily ai_ feed. Add it to Chrome.

I didn't expect a song to make the week's best argument about AI. Michael Harley built a Claude Code skill that finds the Bandcamp page of music he hears on Instagram, so he can buy it. When Bandcamp added a challenge to its search, Claude refused to help him get past it. It would still download the same track from YouTube, where the band earns nothing 🎸. His conclusion: LLM agents work for their parent companies, not for you.

The rest of the week asked the same question: whose side is your agent on: Google's new Workspace terms send every agent through its own MCP servers, where Anthropic's Claude connector can send your email and an independent developer's agent can only leave drafts. Signal will block assistants like Gemini and Siri from reading its chats off the screen, and Character.AI's bankruptcy sale puts its users' private chats up for auction. An agent is only as loyal as the company that runs it. I'd read the terms before handing one the keys.
