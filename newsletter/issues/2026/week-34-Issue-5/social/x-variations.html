The teardown

Ito - AI code review that runs your code
PRODUCTHUNT · https://www.producthunt.com/products/ito-ai-code-review-that-runs-code
What the sandbox replaces, what it costs, who should skip it.
6 tweets
1
Ito, an AI code reviewer that boots your app in a disposable environment and clicks through the flows your PR touches before it says a word about the diff. 445 upvotes on Product Hunt. The interesting part is what the sandbox replaces.
236/280
2
Diff-only review, human or model, is prediction. You read code and guess what the runtime will do. Most incidents live in the gap: the migration that locks a table, the config that breaks under real routing, the contract that changed two services away.
253/280
3
Ito's move: run the app, exercise the touched flows, attach runtime evidence to the review. 'This looks wrong' becomes 'this broke, here, when I did this.'
156/280
4
The cost. Your app must boot hermetically: no prod data, no real third-party creds. For many teams that alone is a quarter of unfinished platform work. And every PR pays the boot time.
185/280
5
The quieter limit: a flow validator only catches what it exercises. If the broken path is not in the validated set, runtime evidence is silence. Skip it if your bottleneck is design arguments. No sandbox settles architecture.
226/280
