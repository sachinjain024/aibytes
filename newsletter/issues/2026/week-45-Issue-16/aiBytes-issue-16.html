Welcome back to your weekly dose of AI Bytes. I was braced for a week of model launches, and the story that stayed with me was an editor extension. A backdoored build of the Claude Code extension sat on the Cursor Marketplace, opening a reverse SSH tunnel on the machines of developers who installed it 🪤

The models are fine, it's everything around them: GPT-6.1 Terra, Gemini 4 Carbon and the open weights of Mistral Large 4 all shipped, but the stories builders argued about were about trust. Cohere caught Alibaba's Qwen model trying to send a file to Beijing, a gist showed Claude Code sending each background agent's transcript to its model, and court filings put Stanford professors' pirated books in Claude's training data. Nobody is going to stop using these tools, but this was the week to start reading what you install.
