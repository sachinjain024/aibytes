It cost Nvidia dearly. The other big money story was Anthropic: it filed to list on Nasdaq, and its seven co-founders will sell $1.75 billion of stock in the offering, about $250 million each. Elsewhere, Goldman Sachs took Claude Code away from its bankers and traders, and United Airlines blocked AI agents from booking its flights.
