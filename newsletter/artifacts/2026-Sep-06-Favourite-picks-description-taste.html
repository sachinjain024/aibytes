Giving Picks a Second Line

The Aug-30 sheet locked My Favourite Picks as bare single lines for one reason: a shortlist that carries context stops being a route map and becomes a fifth content bucket sitting on top of News for Devs. Adding a description reopens exactly that. So the frame stays fixed - ink border, signal spine, highlighted numerals, grey provenance chip - and only the interior of the row moves. The three picks and their descriptions are identical in all three; each specimen is followed by the greyed-back News card that sits beneath it, because that is where duplication shows up.

V1 - Hanging note

Why this one. It adds the context without changing what the section is. The description sits on its own row, so the title still reads alone on a downward scan and the shortlist stays a route map rather than something you have to read. V2 was the cheapest to build and the most compact, but running the gloss on from the title adopts the exact grammar of Launches, GitHub and HN - the picks would have become a fourth ticker section, which is the outcome the Aug-30 verdict spent itself avoiding. V3 defended the section's distinctness hardest, but bought that defence with a new label token and a standing obligation to write three lines in voice every week.

Register: neutral summary. The description says what the item is, not why it was picked. Length: up to two lines, about 155 characters.

The trade to watch. A neutral summary is, by construction, close to what the item's home row already says - pick 01's description and its News row now sit two scrolls apart saying adjacent things. The numeral, the chip and the hanging indent are the whole of what keeps this reading as an index rather than a duplicate. If descriptions start restating home rows verbatim, the register is the thing to revisit, not the layout.

Two calls, as answered

1. Register Summary

Independent of the layout, and settled in favour of the summary: the picks stay informational, and the publisher's voice stays where it already lives, in the intro. The reason register is kept on file below in case the duplication trade above starts to bite.

SummaryArchitecture and sequence diagrams that export clean, driven as an agent skill.
ReasonIt replaced two diagramming tools for me this week, and the exports actually survive a resize.

2. Length Up to two lines

Settled at two. At 640px a description runs about 78 characters per line, so a two-line note is roughly 155 characters and the card grows by about 20px per pick. Three picks at two lines each adds ~60px above the fold, which pushes News for Devs down on a phone. The allowance is a ceiling, not a target - issue #8's three descriptions run 77, 62 and 59 characters, so the card grows by one line per pick rather than two.
