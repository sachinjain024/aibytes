N1 Official Announcements · L3 Twin card · P1 after AI News

Why this combination. The top of the issue becomes two cards in the same language: AI News for what happened, then Official Announcements for what the companies shipped, in their own words. The name says exactly what the card holds, and keeping it as a card rather than a numbered section lets it sit right under AI News without breaking the 0x sequence.

Amended on selection: the whole post, word for word. Each entry is the company name in bold, linked to the post on X, the posting account in grey, the official page the post links to, and then the entire post exactly as written, line breaks included. No release name or headline of ours, and no cut: this week's announcements run 88 to 403 characters, and NVIDIA's model is only named in its second paragraph. The long-post rule from Loudest on X (past 600 characters, cut at the author's own paragraph break and link to the rest) applies only if a post runs that long. Entries are in likes order, with no stat shown.

Styled as S3, inset statements. Each entry is a byline (company in bold, then the posting account and day in grey mono), the whole post in a paper-coloured inset with the same border and radius as the quote at the foot of AI News, and a mono footer: Post on X ↗ · anthropic.com ↗. The inset is what marks the words as the company's, not ours. The card has a 1.5px ink border and the subtitle "This week's launches, in the companies' own words."

Numbers that follow from it. Both cards are unnumbered, so Loudest on X is 0x02, then Products 0x03, GitHub 0x04, HN 0x05. AI News loses launch stories to this card, so its story of the week goes to the best remaining story.

P1 keeps the issue's shape; P2 leads with the launches. Moving launches out of AI News takes its usual story of the week with them (this week, Opus 5.5), so in P1 AI News opens with the best remaining story and the launches follow straight after. P2 puts them first, but a numbered header above the AI News card would look like it owns that card, so P2 only works with L3. P1 works with any of the three. Chosen: L3 in P1, two cards back to back, so Loudest on X becomes 0x02.

Knock-on changes, whichever wins

  1. Section numbers shift. Products, GitHub and HN each move down one or two. The template, the Beehiiv patterns and the export template all carry the numbers, and they change together in the template step.
  2. The Today line in the intro gains two entries, and the intro may still mention a launch in its storyline. That's allowed: the rule is that AI News drops the story, not the intro.
  3. The company label in L1 is the posting account; in L2 and L3 it is the company (Logan Kilpatrick's post files under Google, SpaceXAI under xAI). That matches the contract's "all of a company's accounts count as one".
