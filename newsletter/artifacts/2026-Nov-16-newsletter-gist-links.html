Newsletter gist links

The destinations are a product decision (F2). This note only records that the one-colour Substack mapping was applied, without a new numbered-options round.

16 Nov 2026 · AIB-145y T35 · spec docs/specs/AIB-145y-newsletter-gist-links.md · Decide 1, settled as recommended

Issue HTML keeps two colours: cobalt on the title (the gist), grey .src on the trailing source. UTM tags live only on the gist URL: utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-N.

Substack has one accent. Two orange links per row is the cost of F2: the title still has to be the gist, and the source still has to be reachable. HN Deep titles stay unbolded (Issue 17) and become links. Viral on X keeps the words as body ink; the handle goes to the gist and Post on X trails to the post. Issues 1–18 have no gist hrefs, so they still export as they do today.
