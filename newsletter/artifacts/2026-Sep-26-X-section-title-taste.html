Naming the X section

Each option is shown in the newsletter's own section header, with its subtitle, one sample row, and the way the section would appear in the intro's Today: line. The name has to work in both places. From round 1 you kept 03 and 05. Round 2 adds three names in the same direction: plain, and ranked by engagement. The 0x05 number is a placeholder, since placement is decided in step 7. Sample rows are placeholders, not real posts.

Decision

Loudest on X

Subtitle: The AI posts that drew the biggest reaction this week. Intro phrasing: the loudest posts on X.

Why: the name says exactly how posts are chosen, which is by engagement and nothing else. aiBytes_ does not claim these are the best or most insightful posts, only the ones that got the biggest reaction. At three words it is short, it names X, and it reads naturally in the intro. It won over 03 Top AI Posts on X, the other finalist, because it is more memorable. The subtitle keeps "AI" and "this week" out of the header.

Watch for: "loud" can suggest hot takes. The subtitle frames it as reaction, and the posts are quoted exactly as written, so the reader can judge them.
