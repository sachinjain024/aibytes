Why this one. It is the only frame that admits the picks come from three different sections of the issue. The trade to watch: nothing but the cursor now signals that a pick is clickable, so the section leans on readers understanding that a shortlist is a list of links.

Two calls to make with it

Where it sits. All three specimens assume the section goes directly after the intro, above News for Devs - the shortlist is the TL;DR of a TL;DR issue, and burying it under four sections wastes it. The cost is the duplication you can see in the greyed card: two of your four picks are also the top two news rows. Putting it last instead turns it into a recap and removes the collision. Say which you want.

Whether it takes a hex number. News for Devs is a card with no 0x number; Launches, GitHub and HN are numbered sections. If picks stay a card, nothing shifts. If it becomes a numbered section it takes 0x02 and pushes Launches, GitHub and HN to 0x03, 0x04 and 0x05.
