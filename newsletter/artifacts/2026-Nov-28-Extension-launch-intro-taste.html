Where the Chrome extension goes in Issue #20

Four ways to introduce the aiBytes_ extension to readers, shown in the issue's own styles. Pick a number, or tell me what to change in one.

Chosen: option 2 (publisher, 28 Nov 2026). The publisher reworded it on this page to the version shown. The launch paragraph goes right after the standing welcome line, before the hook. The welcome line becomes its own paragraph. Claude had recommended option 1; the publisher chose to lead with the launch. The extension was live on the Chrome Web Store by then.
