How each issue is done:
1. Convert the repo's `aiBytes-issue-N.html` to the Substack layout (`.claude/skills/generate-newsletter-content/references/substack-layout.md`).
2. Title and subtitle come from the Beehiiv posts export; the social image is the thumbnail Beehiiv used (Issues 1-3 had none).
3. Publish on the **web only**. "Send via email" is unticked and checked before Publish; no subscriber gets an email.
4. Backdate under the post's Settings → Displayed publication date to the Sunday of that issue's week, 17:29 IST.

Each item is ticked once its post is live, with its URL as a comment.
