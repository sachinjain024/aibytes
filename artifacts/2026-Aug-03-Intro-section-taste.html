What the pick locked in

The handle is "builders." It's fixed copy in the template now, not a per-issue decision, and it agrees with the News for devs heading. Changing it later means changing it everywhere at once.

The weekday comes from the issue date. "Monday-morning" is a promise about the send day - true for #3, wrong the moment an issue ships on a Tuesday. The skill fills {{WEEKDAY}} from date +%A rather than hard-coding it.

No counts in the intro. Never a comment total, upvote tally, or star count above the fold: the intro argues, the item rows carry the receipts. Where the old copy leaned on "1,746 comments," it now says what the number meant - "set off the loudest argument of the week."

Single hyphens only. No em dashes or en dashes anywhere in the output - prose, item rows, ranges, or subject lines. A spaced hyphen (" - ") does the job. The specimens above are shown with the corrected punctuation, and SKILL.md carries the rule so the generator stops reaching for the long one.

"Let's dive in." is retired. The Today: menu is the closer, and it gets rewritten every issue to match the actual section lineup.

The old rule is gone. SKILL.md used to say the intro takes "never a salutation - no 'Hey crew', 'Hi everyone', or any greeting." That line is now the four-beat pattern above, so future issues open this way by default.

Five files carry it. aiBytes-issue-3.html and the export's Part 1 block for this issue; assets/template.html, references/beehiiv-patterns.html and SKILL.md for every issue after it.
