Settled

01, the butter notch, at weight 500. It is the only option that solves the darkness without giving up the notch, and the notch is the one piece of shape in the whole issue that is not a rectangle. Keeping the signal yellow on the pivotal word means the closing block and the "Story of the week" badge speak the same language, so the yellow reads as a system rather than a one-off.

The idea text dropped from 700 to 500, the lightest weight the issue actually loads for Bricolage Grotesque, with line-height opened to 1.55 to suit it. The highlighted pivot word stays at 700, so the emphasis now comes from weight and colour together instead of a whole paragraph shouting. Every specimen above is shown at the shipping weight.

Runners-up, for the record: 04 if it should be quieter still, 02 if it should recede into the issue.
