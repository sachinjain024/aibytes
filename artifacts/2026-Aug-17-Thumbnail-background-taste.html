DECISION RECORD · aiBytes_ · 2026-08-17 · THUMBNAIL BACKGROUND

Five backgrounds for the thumbnail card

The plain paper card read as unfinished, so five background treatments were rendered from the real Issue 5 headline through the real Chrome pipeline. All five keep the ink frame, the notch, the wordmark row and the highlight untouched; each paints only behind the type and fades to plain paper where the headline sits.

VERDICT

Not one background - three. Dot grid, cobalt wash and graph grid all ship: every run of the skill now renders all three into the issue's thumbnails/ folder as issue-{num}-thumbnail-{variant}.png, and the one that goes to Beehiiv is picked at upload time, issue by issue. Ghost hex (Claude's recommendation) and notch stripes were not picked. Variants live in assets/thumbnail.html as .card::before rules gated by a class on body; the script's VARIANTS tuple is the roster.

0Plain paperBASELINE

What every issue shipped with through Issue 5's first render. Nothing behind the headline.

1Dot gridCHOSEN body.dot-grid

Slate dots faded to nothing behind the headline. Engineer's notebook; the quietest treatment.

2Graph gridCHOSEN body.graph-grid

Hairline squares, a stronger rule every fourth line, calm at the centre. More structure than the dots.

3Ghost hexNOT PICKED

The issue tag set enormous and outlined, bottom-right. Claude's recommendation for its per-issue identity; not picked.

4Cobalt washCHOSEN body.cobalt-wash

A cobalt glow off the notch corner answered by signal yellow bottom-left. The only treatment that adds colour.

5Notch stripesNOT PICKED

45-degree hairlines falling away from the cut corner. Made the notch the story; not picked.
