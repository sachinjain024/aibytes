V2 - I didn't expect this

Why this one. It keeps the publisher in the paragraph without making the paragraph about the publisher. The "I" is reacting to the news, not narrating the draft - so the warmth of the first-person voice survives, but the reader is never walked through an outline of a week that didn't happen. "A week in AI thinking about paper" states the whole issue in eight words and hands off to the story in the same breath. V1 was shorter but spent the person entirely; V3 kept the setup-and-turn that was the problem in the first place.

What repeats is the shape, not the words. The beat is surprise as a reaction, then the story that caused it - "I did not expect…" is one phrasing of it, and the phrasing changes every issue. The banned shape is explicit in the skill now: no more "I had this issue half-written as X - then Z".
